Our Heritage & Craft

More than a drink.

Boba Bloom was born in the heart of Cotonou from a simple longing: to unite authentic Taiwanese tea traditions with the vibrant, sun-drenched spirit of coastal West Africa.

We don't do artificial syrups or pre-packaged powder mixes. Every morning, our team begins by brewing loose-leaf black teas, jasmine blossoms, and ceremonial matcha in small batches. Our tapioca pearls are hand-stirred in slow-reduced dark brown sugar for over 90 minutes until they achieve the quintessential bouncy chew.
